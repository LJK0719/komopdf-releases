## What's new in komopdf 0.1.19

- Use komopdf in 11 interface languages across web and desktop.
- Switch languages without losing your open PDF, selection, zoom or text draft.
- Get localized application menus, installer prompts, settings and release notes.
- Improved protection for unsaved work when leaving an editing tool, and corrected quit cancellation on macOS.

Available for Windows x64 and macOS Apple Silicon (macOS 13 or later).
