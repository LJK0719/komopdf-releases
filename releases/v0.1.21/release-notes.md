# komopdf 0.1.21

- Open long PDFs faster, with more responsive scrolling and zooming.
- More reliable text and object selection, including selections across pages.
- Fixes for zoom position, comments, forms, and continuing to edit newly inserted text.
- Mac: fixes image resizing during export and improves Chinese italics.
- Smaller desktop downloads, with offline editing, text recognition, Word conversion and komo still included.

---

- 长篇 PDF 打开更快，滚动和缩放响应更流畅。
- 改善文字和对象的首次选择，以及跨页文字选择。
- 修复缩放位置、批注、表单及插入文字后继续编辑的问题。
- Mac 版修复导出时缩小图片的问题，并改善中文斜体显示。
- 减小桌面版下载体积，继续保留离线编辑、文字识别、Word 转换和 komo。

Windows supports offline installation. Mac requires Apple Silicon and macOS 13 or later.
